Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state located in southern India. Its capital, Bengaluru, is known as one of India's major technology and IT hubs.

Karnataka is famous for its rich history, culture, traditions, beautiful landscapes and delicious cuisine.



Facts

  • Rich history and heritage
  • Beautiful places like Hampi, Mysuru and Coorg
  • Famous for Yakshagana and Carnatic music
  • Beautiful beaches, waterfalls and forests
  • Major centre for technology and education
