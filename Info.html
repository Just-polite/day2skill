Travel Information

How to Reach Karnataka

By Air

Karnataka has airports in Bengaluru, Mangaluru, Mysuru and Hubballi.

By Train

Karnataka has a good railway network connecting major cities and tourist places.

By Road

Karnataka is well connected by roads and highways. Buses, taxis and private vehicles are available.

Best Time to Visit

October to February is generally suitable for sightseeing. Monsoon is a good time to enjoy greenery and waterfalls.

Travel Tips

  • Check the weather before travelling.
  • Carry water and comfortable footwear.
  • Follow safety rules at tourist places.
  • Respect local culture and monuments.
